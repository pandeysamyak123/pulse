The rule: never overwrite Arjun

The pipeline writes accounts only through apply_signal_update(), which sets system columns. Guard triggers refuse any write to his fields, contacts, notes or history unless it comes from his own RPCs. A hash of all his data is taken before and after every scan; the Runs page re-attacks it live (7/7 blocked).

Why LinkedIn is hand-off only

LinkedIn’s terms forbid scraping and automated messaging, and automation risks banning Arjun’s account. Executive moves are caught through news; Pulse drafts the note within LinkedIn’s 280-character limit, copies it and opens the profile. Arjun sends it and taps Mark as sent.

The Cut: AI judges, code decides

Gemini reads the news and writes; it never sets the score or sends anything. Warmth is a deterministic formula Arjun can open and check. Drafts that invent a figure, flatter, or skip the trigger are regenerated once and stay flagged. SAFE_MODE keeps every outreach email in the demo inbox.
